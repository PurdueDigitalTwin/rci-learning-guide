Driving through an RCI

Preview

Coming from a side road? To turn left or reach the road across the highway, turn right first and follow the signs to the U-turn.

To turn left

1. Turn rightFollow the STOP or YIELD sign. Check traffic and enter when it is safe.
2. Make the U-turnFollow the signs, change lanes when it is safe, and turn at the signed opening.
3. ContinueYou are now heading in the opposite direction. Continue on the highway.

To go across the highway

Turn right, make the signed U-turn, then come back toward the intersection. Move into the right lane when it is safe and turn right onto the side road.

To turn right

Follow the sign at the entrance, check traffic, and turn right when it is safe. Continue on the highway. You do not need the U-turn.

Along the way

  • Look ahead for the U-turn signs and lane markings.
  • Signal and check your mirrors and blind spot before each lane change.
  • At the U-turn, follow the posted sign and wait for enough room to turn safely.
  • At night or in poor weather, reduce speed and leave extra space.

Signs and lane arrangements vary. Follow the signs at the intersection you are using.
